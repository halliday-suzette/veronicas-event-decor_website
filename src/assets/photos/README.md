appears on the site automatically (optimized to WebP, lazy-loaded); until then a wood-tone
placeholder is shown. Alt text for each photo is in `src/data/photos.ts` — check it matches the
real photo.

Never add stock or AI-generated images, photos with licensed characters (e.g. Winnie the Pooh),
or photos showing a child's name without the family's OK.
